# Code of Conduct

Cove welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and levels of experience. Treat people with respect and discuss ideas on their merits.

Be constructive, acknowledge mistakes, respect privacy, and provide useful context when asking for help. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing private information are unacceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Enforcement should be proportionate and consistent, and reports should be handled discreetly.

To report a conduct concern, contact a maintainer using a contact method listed on their GitHub profile. If no private contact is listed, open an issue requesting a private contact without including sensitive details. Do not publish another person's personal information in a report.
