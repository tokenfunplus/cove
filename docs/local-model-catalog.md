# Small-model catalog source notes

Reviewed: 2026-11-22

The discovery list contains 31 selected artifact sets across eleven uses, all within 500 MB. It is bundled metadata and works without contacting a model hub. BGE Small Chinese, Qwen2.5 0.5B, Qwen3 0.6B Abliterated Q5_K_M, and the five managed speech bundles are integrated in Cove. Explore-only entries are candidates to inspect, not installed capabilities or verified recommendations.

Source sizes were checked against model-hub file metadata or maintainer download pages. Hugging Face source links pin the reviewed revision. Standalone maintainer pages remain live references. Sizes exclude supporting runtimes, tokenizers/dictionaries, and working memory; compressed archives need additional space after extraction.

## Interpretation

- OCR includes both the selected detector and recognizer. OPUS-MT includes the encoder and merged decoder. Kokoro includes its network and one listed voice vector, not every available voice.
- Multilingual reranking and embeddings require their own tokenizer, pooling/prefix rules, and new indexes; selecting a source does not replace Cove's BGE index.
- LFM uses the LFM Open License, and SenseVoice uses a custom FunASR model agreement. Runtime licenses do not establish model-weight licensing.
- The publisher cards currently declare Apache-2.0 for OPUS-MT EN-ZH and CC-BY-4.0 for ZH-EN; the catalog preserves this distinction.
- Small chat and coding models remain experimental. No ranking or general quality claim is implied by inclusion.

See [ADR-0051](adr/0051-expand-small-model-discovery-by-task.md) for presentation and integration boundaries.
