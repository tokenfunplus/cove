# Cove 0.1.0-beta.13

Beta 13 adds interactive replies to Chat and the Quick Panel. A model can offer a card alongside its answer so you can rank alternatives, adjust a plan, or practice a quiz in the conversation.

## Try an interactive reply

- Choose one eligible external model and ask, “Help me divide a $500 weekend budget between travel, food, and activities. Give me sliders and show how much is left.” Cards are optional and depend on the model. Other examples to try include ranking trip ideas or taking a quiz one question at a time.
- Drag items to change their order, adjust sliders to see a running total, or edit text with a live preview. Quiz cards can reveal a hint or check an answer. These actions run locally, and Cove saves your edits on this Mac.
- Choose **Expand** for more room. Built-in local models, model comparisons, and external models advertising a context window below 8,192 tokens continue to use ordinary replies.

## Follow up on a card

- Choose **Continue** to send the displayed values to the selected model. To ask about unfinished choices, use **Ask about this** on the card.
- To discuss a card in an ordinary message, select **Include card** in the composer. It starts unchecked. You can add files to the same message and use **Review context** to inspect what Cove will send. The preview runs locally.
- After you send the card, its submitted values are locked. Later turns use a compact card summary, and the original reply stays in your conversation history.

See the [interactive replies guide](https://github.com/tokenfunplus/cove/blob/v0.1.0-beta.13/docs/user-guide.md#try-interactive-replies).

## Download and upgrade

- Requires an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 13 or later. Local inference and speech require macOS 13.3 or later.
- Download `Cove-0.1.0-beta.13-arm64.dmg`. Quit Cove, open the disk image, drag Cove to Applications, and relaunch it.
- This is build `13`, with marketing version `0.1.0`. Cove upgrades its SQLite database to schema 29 to store saved card values and submissions. If you plan to return to an earlier build, quit Cove and back up your data before upgrading.
- Choose **Check for Updates > Check Beta Releases…** to discover this prerelease. Verify the download using its SHA-256 checksum and [detached publisher signatures](https://github.com/tokenfunplus/cove/blob/v0.1.0-beta.13/docs/releasing.md#detached-release-signatures).
