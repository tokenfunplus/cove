# Cove 0.1.0-beta.10

Beta 10 adds the native macOS Quick Panel and improves download recovery, local document search, and feedback during on-device generation.

## Quick Panel

- Choose **Open Quick Panel** from the Cove menu bar or enable **Quick Panel Shortcut** under **Launcher Settings**. Press **Control-Option-Space** to open a compact window with the composer focused.
- Type, paste text, or drop a supported file. The window expands for longer drafts and answers. Manual window sizing applies for the current Launcher session.
- Pin the panel to keep it nearby. **Escape** hides it with the draft and attachments available when you reopen it.
- The composer shows the selected model and processing destination. Find extra actions under **Tools**. Scroll up to hold your reading position during a reply, or choose **Jump to latest** to follow new text.
- After the turn settles, choose **Continue in Cove** to open the saved conversation in your browser. The draft stays in the panel during Browser Session renewal.

See the [Quick Panel guide](../user-guide.md#use-the-quick-panel) and [native window screenshots](../images/README.md).

## Local capability improvements

- Retry interrupted inference and speech downloads from validated partial files. Cove still verifies the complete artifact against its pinned checksum before installation.
- Local document search groups overlapping results by original section and offers a similarity filter with guidance for empty results.
- On-device chat shows waiting, preparation, and generation status. Switching installed chat models releases the previous model's working memory.
- Speech preview uses unsaved volume and CPU settings. Speech drafts stay available when switching capability views.
- Context review shows the local output budget. Each local attempt records the effective token limit that was applied.

## Download and upgrade

- Requires an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 13 or later. Local inference and speech require macOS 13.3 or later.
- Download `Cove-0.1.0-beta.10-arm64.dmg`. Quit Cove, open the disk image, drag Cove to Applications, and relaunch it.
- This is build `10`, with marketing version `0.1.0`. Beta 10 uses SQLite schema 27 and macOS Keychain for credentials.
- **Check for Beta Updates** can find this prerelease. Verify the download using the attached SHA-256 checksum and [detached publisher signatures](../releasing.md#detached-release-signatures).
